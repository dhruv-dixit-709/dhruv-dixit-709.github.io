01 Background

I'm an AI Researcher at the Society-Centered AI Lab (SAIL) in Chapel Hill, North Carolina. My work asks how small language models can reason accurately, retrieve evidence faithfully, and retain useful skills as they adapt.

I completed an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Stevens Institute of Technology, where I worked with Dr. Min Song on executable reasoning for language models. Before that, I earned an M.S. in Physics and a B.E. at BITS Pilani.

My research connects reinforcement learning, tool-augmented inference, evidence verification, and continual adaptation. I'm especially interested in practical models that operate with limited compute and dependable feedback.

02 Selected research

My work centers on three questions: how models learn to execute reasoning, how they verify external information, and how they preserve knowledge over time.
