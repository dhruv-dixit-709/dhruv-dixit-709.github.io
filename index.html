01 Background

I'm an AI researcher at the Society-Centered AI Lab (SAIL) at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, under Dr. Neil Gaikwad ↗. I study how small language models can become more capable and reliable while using limited computation.

I completed an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Stevens Institute of Technology, where I worked with Dr. Min Song ↗ on executable reasoning for language models. I previously earned an M.S. in Physics and a B.E. at BITS Pilani.

My interests include reinforcement learning, tool-augmented reasoning, continual learning, and evidence-grounded NLP. I focus on practical approaches that can be evaluated for accuracy, reliability, and computational efficiency.
